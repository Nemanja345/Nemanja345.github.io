Frequently asked questions

How do free PC game giveaways work?

Stores such as the Epic Games Store, Steam, GOG and others occasionally give a game away for a limited time. If you claim it before the offer ends, it is added to your library and stays yours to keep, even after the price goes back up.

Are these games free forever?

No. Most giveaways last from a few hours to about a week. Claim a game while it shows as free; after the end date the normal price returns. The end date is shown on each card when the store provides it.

Do I need to pay anything or enter a credit card?

Claiming a free game does not cost anything. Some stores ask you to sign in or create a free account first.

How can I get notified when a game becomes free?

Install the Game Sniper app for Android, add the games you are waiting for to your price alerts, and you will get a notification when one goes on sale or becomes free.

Which stores are covered?

Steam, Epic Games Store, GOG, Humble Store and many other PC game stores. Prices are shown in US dollars and can differ by region.
